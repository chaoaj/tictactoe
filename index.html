How to play

  • Mouse or touch: click or tap a cell to place your mark.
  • Keyboard: Tab to the board, use the arrow keys or number keys 1-9 to move focus, then press Enter or Space to play.
  • Press N for a new round, R to reset the score, and B to hear a description of the board.
  • Get three marks in a row, column, or diagonal to win.
